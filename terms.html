1. What MediaMentor is

MediaMentor reviews social media posts before you publish them and gives you a

score, flags anything worth a second look, and suggests rewrites.

It is a coach, not an approval system. We give you an opinion. You decide

what to post.

2. Your account

You must be at least 13 to use MediaMentor. If you are 13 to 17, your club

must have your parent's or guardian's permission before adding you.

Keep your password to yourself. Tell us if you think someone else has it.

One account per person. Do not let someone else use yours.

3. Your content

Your posts stay yours. You own the captions, photos and videos you upload.

We do not claim any ownership of them.

You give us permission to store your content and send it to OpenAI for analysis,

for as long as we need to do that and no longer. That permission ends when you

delete the content or your account.

You promise that the content you upload is yours to upload, that you have

permission from anyone identifiable in it, and that it does not break the law.

4. What you may not do

No objectionable content and no abuse. We have zero tolerance for either.

That means no harassment, bullying, threats or hate towards anyone; nothing

sexual, violent or degrading; and nothing that targets a teammate, an opponent,

an official or a fan.

You also may not:

  • Upload someone else's content as your own.
  • Upload anything illegal, or any sexual content involving a minor.
  • Try to break, overload, or reverse-engineer the service.
  • Use MediaMentor to review content for a business that has not paid for it.
  • Resell or redistribute the service.

Anyone can report a post or hide a person's posts from inside the app. We read

reports and act on them, usually within 24 hours. We can remove content, and

suspend or close an account, that breaks these rules.

5. Clubs and organisations

If your club provides your access, your club can see the posts you publish to

its feed, and can set rules that affect how your posts are reviewed.

Your club cannot see the reviews you do not publish. That is enforced by our

database, not by a setting.

If your club stops paying, you may lose access to club features. Your own

account and your own review history remain yours.
