Deleting your MediaMentor account

In the app (immediate): Profile → Settings → Delete my account, then

confirm.

Without the app: email mediamentorsupport@gmail.com from the address

you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it

within 7 days and reply to confirm. If you cannot send from that address, tell

us and we will check it is really you before deleting anything.

What is deleted

Everything, straight away: your login, your profile, every review you ran,

every post you published (so your posts leave your club's feed), every report

you made, and every photo and video you uploaded. Our backups roll off within 7

days, after which no copy remains. OpenAI deletes its copy of anything you had

reviewed within 30 days under its API terms.

What is not deleted

Your club, its rules and its other members' posts carry on without you. If you

ran a club, ask another admin to take it over first, or email us.

Deletion cannot be undone.
