MediaMentor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who we are: JBLZ Pty Ltd, trading as MediaMentor, Australia.

Contact: mediamentorsupport@gmail.com

The short version

  • We hold your email, your name, your sport, your club, the posts you check, and

the photos and videos you attach.

  • To review a post, we send it to OpenAI in the United States. They return

a result and do not train their models on it.

  • Our database and file storage are in Mumbai, India, with our hosting

provider Supabase.

  • Your reviews are private. Your club cannot see them, not even an admin.

Only posts you choose to publish are visible to your club.

  • Photos and videos are deleted 90 days after you upload them, unless they

are on a post you published to your club.

  • You can delete your account from inside the app, and it deletes everything.
  • We do not sell your data, we do not advertise, and we run no trackers or

analytics.

1. What we collect

Your account. Your email address and a password. The password is stored

hashed by our authentication provider; we never see it.

Your profile. Your display name, your sport if you give one, your club, and

whether you are a club admin.

Posts you check. The caption you write, the platform you are posting to, and

any photo or video you attach. For a video, we also turn what is said in it into

text, so the review can take it into account.

What the review produces. A score, a status (good to go, worth a look, or

hold off), notes on anything worth attention with a suggested fix, a suggested

rewrite, and a one-word topic for the post. Notes can record that a post

mentioned things like alcohol, gambling or a sponsor's competitor.

Reports and hidden people. If you report a post, we keep the report and the

reason. If you hide someone's posts, we keep that choice so it sticks.

If you run a club. Your club's name and rules (banned words, sponsors,

policies), and the email addresses of anyone you invite.

Location. Photos and videos can carry hidden information about where they

were taken. We remove location from photos before they leave your phone where

the photo's format allows it. We never read or use location, and anything left

in a file is deleted with the file.

What we do not collect. No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no

contacts, no browsing history, no payment details, and no third-party trackers.

We do not ask your age (see section 7).

2. What we use it for

To review your posts, show you your own history, and show your club the posts

you publish. That is the whole purpose.

We do not use your content to train any model, ours or anyone else's, and we do

not sell or rent it to anyone.

3. Who else sees it

OpenAI, in the United States. To review a post we send OpenAI the caption,

the photo, still frames and the soundtrack from a video, and your club's rules.

We do not send your name, your email address or your file names. Under

OpenAI's API terms, this content is not used to train their models; they keep

it for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse, then delete it.

That is a disclosure of your information overseas. The United States has privacy

laws that differ from Australia's. There is no version of this product that

reviews a post without sending it somewhere to be read, so if you are not

comfortable with this, please do not use the app.

Our review server. Your post passes through our own server, run by Railway,

on its way to OpenAI. It keeps no copy: a video is processed in temporary

storage that is deleted as soon as the review is done. Its logs record that a

review happened and for which account, never what the post said.

Your club. Only what you choose to publish. Your club sees the published

caption, its photo, its score and status, your display name and your sport.

Club admins also see the email addresses of their members. **Your club cannot

see reviews you did not publish, and cannot see the notes on any review.** This

is enforced by the database itself, not by a setting that could be changed by

accident.

Nobody else. No advertisers, no analytics companies, no data brokers.

4. Where it is stored

Your account, your reviews, your published posts and the files you upload are

stored by our hosting provider, Supabase, in Mumbai, India. That is also an

overseas disclosure under Australian privacy law. Backups are held in the same

region.

When you delete your account, everything is removed from our live systems

straight away: your profile, every review, every published post (so your posts

leave your club's feed), and every file you uploaded. It disappears from our

backups within seven days as they roll off. There is no hidden copy and no

"deactivated" state. Your club, and its rules, carry on without you.

6. Content and safety

Every post is reviewed before it can be published, and a post the review holds

back cannot be published to your club. Anyone can report a post in their club

feed or hide a person's posts; the person who posted is not told. We read

reports and act on them, usually within 24 hours, which can mean removing a

post or closing an account. See our terms.

7. Athletes under 18

MediaMentor is not for anyone under 13. Do not create an account if you are

under 13.

If you are 13 to 17, your club must have your parent's or guardian's

permission before adding you, and is responsible for holding that consent.

If you are a parent and believe your child has an account without your

permission, email us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

You can:

  • See what we hold. Email us and we will send it.
  • Correct it. Your name and sport can be edited in the app; email us for

anything else.

  • Delete it. Profile → Settings → Delete my account, inside the app. Immediate

and complete.

  • Complain. Email us first. If we do not resolve it, you can complain to the

Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

We will respond to any request within 30 days.

9. Security

Database-level access rules make another person's data unreachable, not merely

hidden. Reviews are private to their author, including from club admins. All

traffic is encrypted in transit. The keys that reach our systems are held on our

servers and are never shipped inside the app.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If there is a

data breach that affects you, we will tell you and notify the OAIC, as the

Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will tell you in the app

before it takes effect.
