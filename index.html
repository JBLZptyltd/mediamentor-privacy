MediaMentor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

MediaMentor helps athletes check a social post before they publish it. This explains what we hold, why, and who else sees it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

  • We hold your email address, your display name, and the posts you ask us to review.
  • Your reviews are private to you. Your club admin cannot read them. Nobody can.
  • Only a post you choose to publish becomes visible to your club.
  • To review a post, we send it to OpenAI. They process it and do not train on it.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at any time.

What we collect

Your account. Your email address and password, handled by our authentication provider, Supabase. We never see your password; it is stored hashed and we cannot read it.

Your profile. Your display name, your sport if you give one, your club, and whether you are a club admin.

What you ask us to review. The caption you write, the platform you picked, and any photo, video clip or voice note you attach. Alongside it we keep the review itself: the score, the flags, and the suggested rewrite.

What you publish. If you choose to share a post, we keep the final caption, the image, the score and the time you published it.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your device identifiers for advertising, or any analytics about how you use the app. There is no advertising in MediaMentor and no third-party tracking.

Reviewing a post and deciding not to publish it costs you nothing and tells nobody. That is the point of the app. It is enforced in the database itself rather than by the app being polite about it — the rows are unreachable, not merely unrequested.

Sending your content to OpenAI

To review a post, we send it to OpenAI, who run the model that reads it.

What goes to them: your caption, and if you attached media, still frames from your clip or photo, plus a text transcription of any audio. Your club's rules go too, so the model can check the post against them.

What does not go to them: your email address, your name, your club's name, or anything identifying you. The request carries the content and nothing about who wrote it.

What they do with it: process the request and return a result. Under OpenAI's API terms, content sent through the API is not used to train their models. OpenAI retains API request data for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, then deletes it.

The models used are OpenAI's gpt-4.1-mini for text and vision, and whisper-1 for transcribing audio.

If you are not comfortable with this, do not attach media and do not submit a caption — there is no way to use the review feature without it, because it is the feature.

Where your data lives

  • Supabase — your account, profile, reviews and published posts. Postgres, with

row-level security enforcing the table above.

  • Supabase Storage — any photo, clip or voice note you attach, in a private

bucket that requires your own login to read.

  • Railway — our review service. It passes content through to OpenAI and does

not store it.

How long we keep it

Your data stays until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry.

Deleting your account removes everything: your profile, every review, every published post, and every file you uploaded. It is immediate and it cannot be undone. Profile → Delete account.

Children

MediaMentor is not intended for under-13s. If you run a club with younger athletes, they should not have accounts.

Your rights

You can see everything we hold about you inside the app, and you can delete all of it from inside the app. If you would rather ask us, or if you want a copy in a file, contact us at the address below and we will sort it within 30 days.

Under UK and EU data protection law you also have the right to correct inaccurate data, to object to processing, and to complain to your data protection regulator.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we do with your content, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
